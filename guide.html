THE BUILDER'S
CODEX

Builder OS isn't a game you finish. It's a game you keep winning, one day at a time — and this is how you play it well.

CH. 00

Start Here

Builder OS is a character sheet for your real life. Instead of a fictional hero, the character is you. Instead of dragons, the challenges are the things you're actually trying to get better at — training, work, school, relationships, money, and how you take care of yourself.

Every day you do things: workouts, deep-work sessions, study, conversations that matter, money moves. Some of those get logged in the app. Logged actions feed six stats. Stats combine into a Builder Level and a title — Apprentice, working up toward Sovereign.

There's no final boss. There's no ending screen. The whole game is: show up more days than you don't, and don't let things slide long enough to decay. That's it. That's the entire strategy guide in one sentence — everything below is detail on how to play it well.

🡒 Getting in

On iPhone: open the dashboard link in Safari, tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen." On Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then "Install app." Either way you get a real home-screen icon that opens full-screen, no browser bar.

🡒 How stats actually move

Every quest (task) you set up is tagged with which stat(s) it trains. Complete it, and that stat gets the points. Some quests train two stats at once. There's no fixed formula tying a life area to a single stat — it depends on what each quest was built to feed.

CH. 02

Domains are the regions of your life — how your quest log is organized, not the stats themselves.

School

Formal learning — courses, study sessions, coursework.

Hustle

Health

Training, sleep, physical upkeep — much of this feeds itself from your watch automatically.

Help

Mentoring, showing up for other people.

Inner

Journaling, reflection, intentional time with people close to you.

Sov

Financial moves, savings, independence-building actions.

CH. 03

Quests: Two Kinds

Every task in your Work Order Log is one of two quest types, and they behave differently.

◇ MILESTONE

A one-off deliverable with a real calendar date. "Submit approval," "finish the assignment." Complete it once, it goes COMPLETE, and it's done — no repeat.

◈ RECURRING

A standing habit with a cadence — DAILY, WEEKLY, FORTNIGHTLY, MONTHLY, or 3X_WEEKLY. It never "finishes." Completing it logs one instance and the quest stays on your list, waiting for next time.

⚠ Don't cross the streams

A RECURRING quest's due-date field is a cadence dropdown, not a date. A MILESTONE's is a real date picker. Pick your Type first — the field only shows correctly once it's set. If you ever see a RECURRING quest showing an empty date-picker instead of a cadence dropdown, don't save it — flag it instead.

  1. Tap + Add Task.
  2. Pick a Domain first — it decides your quest's ID prefix automatically. You never type an ID.
  3. Pick Type before touching the due-date field.
  4. Points for completing late auto-fill at 75% of on-time points. Leave that alone unless this specific quest needs a different penalty.
CH. 04

The Enemy: Decay

Decay is the only real opponent in this game. It's a point penalty that kicks in when a stat's quests go untouched past their grace period.

It exists so that showing up consistently — not one big burst of effort — is what actually moves your level. A huge push this week doesn't cover for silence next week; decay keeps ticking on its own clock.

⚠ If a stat looks stuck

The app can only score what you tell it. If a stat isn't moving despite real effort, it's almost always because the matching quest wasn't marked complete — not because the app failed to notice.

Worked example — the weekly training split. One vague "go to the gym" quest got split into four independent quests, each decaying on its own:

QuestTrainsOn-Time PtsWeekly Decay
Push DaySTR43
Pull DaySTR43
Leg DaySTR43
Run 5KEND33

Each of these runs Monday–Sunday. Skip Leg Day two weeks straight and you eat that penalty twice — independently of whether Push and Pull stayed on track. Splitting a vague habit into specific, separately-tracked quests is a strategy in itself: it stops one strong lift from hiding a weak one.

CH. 05

Reading the HUD

The top of the dashboard is your instant status readout. Here's what each light means.

Hero ring — steady cyan

Everything's nominal. No stat is decaying, nothing's critical.

Hero ring — pulsing amber

At least one stat is currently decaying. Not an emergency, but the clock is running — check the alert banner below it to see which one.

Hero ring — pulsing red

A stat has dropped below 20. This is the "go look now" signal.

Tap any chip in the alert strip and it jumps you straight to that stat's quests in the Work Order Log — no manual searching. The Position — Today panel below it gives you the plain-language version: what's working this week, and what's dragging you down, with clickable links straight to the relevant quests.

CH. 06

Side Resources

Two things get tracked outside the quest log entirely: water and alcohol.

Water — tap the Water tab. The quick buttons (+250ml / +500ml / +750ml) or the custom field add to today's running total; they don't replace it. Every tap moves the bar.

Alcohol — tap the Alcohol tab, log drink type, volume, and ABV. The app tracks running units and an estimated peak BAC automatically for the session — you just log each drink as it happens.

CH. 07

Fast Capture

On desktop, you don't have to open the Add Task form to jot something down.

Hit ⌘K (Mac) or Ctrl K (Windows) anywhere on the dashboard. A quick-capture box pops up — type the quest, hit Enter, done. Esc cancels.

🡒 What happens next

Quick-captured items land at the bottom of your Work Order Log without a Domain, Type, or points yet — they're a placeholder, not a fully set-up quest. Go back and fill those in when you have a minute; that's how they actually start earning stat points.

CH. 10

Quick Reference

Stat
One of six abilities (STR/END/REC/INT/EQ/SOV), 0–100, trained by completing tagged quests.
Domain
One of seven life areas your quests are filed under.
Index
The average of your six stats — the single number your tier is based on.
Decay
A point penalty for leaving a stat's quests untouched past its grace period.
Cadence
How often a RECURRING quest resets — DAILY, WEEKLY, FORTNIGHTLY, MONTHLY, 3X_WEEKLY.
Builder Level / Title
Your overall rank, shown in the hero ring, mapped from your Index.
